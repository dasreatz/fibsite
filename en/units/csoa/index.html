Special Operations Center “A”

Precision. Resilience. Results.

Special Operations Center “A” handles high-complexity assignments and supports other divisions during critical incidents. Its work relies on discipline, training and coordinated action.

Teamwork, discipline and continuous training define the Center’s approach. The division works with other parts of the Bureau when a situation calls for exceptional responsibility and swift coordination of decisions.

Primary responsibilities

  • Special operations and critical incident response
  • Support for interagency operations
  • Protection of civilians and personnel
  • Execution of bureau leadership assignments

About the division

Personnel training covers coordinated action, service procedures, communication and the ability to act responsibly in difficult circumstances. The Center maintains constant contact with other directorates so that they can coordinate tasks and share the necessary information in a timely manner.

Special attention is given to internal discipline, preparation for unusual situations and analysis of the results of completed work. Professional skills matter to the division, but so do trust between employees and compliance with FIB standards.
