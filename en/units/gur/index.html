Intelligence

Main Intelligence Directorate

Know more. Act sooner.

The Main Intelligence Directorate collects and assesses information on potential threats, prepares risk assessments and provides intelligence support to the bureau.

The analytical reports of the division help assess the situation in time and support other directorates. The reliability of information, well-considered conclusions and effective cooperation are at the heart of its work.

Primary responsibilities

  • Information collection and verification
  • Threat analysis and risk assessment
  • Preparation of intelligence reports
  • Support for investigative and special divisions

About the division

Analytical work begins by assessing the reliability of information and comparing different sources. Personnel prepare summaries for management, helping them see the overall picture and take potential risks into account.

Key aspects of the Directorate’s work include confidentiality, precise wording and the timely sharing of verified conclusions with other divisions. This enables coordinated decision-making throughout the Bureau.
