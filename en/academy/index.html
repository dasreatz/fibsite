FIB Academy.

A structured path from application through training to the start of service.

Training stages

01

Application

Register the applicant’s submission and conduct an initial review of the information.

02

Screening

Assessment of motivation, readiness and compliance with the established requirements.

03

Invitation

Approved applicants receive an individual invitation to the next stage.

04

Initial training

Study of procedures, service standards and coordination between divisions.

05

Entry into service

Upon completion of training, personnel begin service in the general corps.

06

Specialization

Further development and eligibility for transfer to a specialized division.
